4. SEMANTIC SIMILARITY
The program uses cosine similarity to retrieve five nearest words.

5. CONCLUSION
Pretrained embeddings allow semantic relationships to be represented numerically.

view of the embedding space, and cosine similarity enables a simple semantic
search system.
